# Phase 01 — Requirements Review

You're reading the user's first draft of `REQUIREMENTS.md`. Your job is
to identify gaps, ambiguities, and contradictions — not to start
planning yet.

## Steps

1. Read `REQUIREMENTS.md` carefully.
2. Identify:
   - **Ambiguities**: things stated vaguely ("fast", "simple", "users")
   - **Missing functional requirements**: implied but not specified
   - **Missing non-functional requirements**: auth, scale, latency,
     compliance, regions, offline support, multi-tenancy
   - **Tech-stack contradictions** with `01-tech-stack.mdc`
   - **Out-of-scope creep**: features that pull the project into a
     different category than its stated goal
3. Write a concise findings report (in chat, not a file) with:
   - 3–8 bullet points of the most consequential gaps
   - 1–3 follow-up questions where you genuinely cannot proceed
     without clarification
4. Do NOT write `PLAN.md` yet. That's phase 02.
5. Do NOT propose solutions for the gaps. Just surface them.
